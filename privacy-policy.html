Privacy Policy

Package: com.pulsenews.app  ·  Last updated: June 2025

Pulse News ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. By using Pulse, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
01

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Phone Number: Collected solely for Firebase Phone OTP Authentication. See Section 5 for full details.
  • Google Account (Name & Email): Collected only if you sign in with Google Sign-In.
  • Topics & Bookmarks: Stored in Firestore for personalisation of your news feed only.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and ads (free users only).
  • Usage Data: Topics you follow, articles you read, and app preferences (theme, language) for analytics via Firebase.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To personalise your news feed based on topics you follow
  • To sync your topics and bookmarks across devices
  • To manage your Premium subscription status
  • To send news notifications (only if you enable them)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
03

Advertising

Pulse uses Google AdMob to show ads to free users. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalised ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data

Premium users do not see any ads. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/privacy.

04

Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase for the following:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure login via Google or phone number (OTP)
  • Cloud Firestore: Storing your topics, bookmarks, and premium status
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Sending push notifications
  • Firebase Analytics: App usage and crash reporting

Firebase's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

05

Phone Number & Authentication Updated

When you choose to sign in using Phone OTP, we collect your phone number solely for Firebase Phone Authentication. The following terms govern how your phone number is handled:

  • Purpose: Phone number is used exclusively to verify your identity via Firebase Phone OTP. It is not used for any other purpose.
  • Storage: Your phone number is not stored beyond what Firebase Authentication inherently requires to maintain your account session.
  • No third-party sharing: Your phone number is never shared with any third party for marketing, advertising, analytics, or any other purpose whatsoever.
  • No marketing use: We will never contact you via your phone number for promotional or marketing communications.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account (and associated phone number) at any time from Settings → Delete My Account.
Note on AI Summaries: Pulse uses the Groq API to generate AI-powered article summaries. This is processed server-side only. Your phone number, name, email, or any personal data is never sent to or shared with the Groq API.
06

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Google's Firebase servers. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. We do not sell your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

07

Data Retention Updated

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service. The following table outlines our retention periods:

Data Type Retention Period
Phone Number Retained until account deletion. Removed from Firebase Auth within 30 days of account deletion.
Google Name & Email Retained until account deletion. Removed from Firebase Auth within 30 days of account deletion.
Topics & Bookmarks Retained until account deletion. All Firestore data permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
Device & Usage Data Retained per Firebase Analytics and AdMob data retention policies (typically 14 months).
Account Deletion: When you delete your account via Settings → Delete My Account, all associated data — including your Firebase Authentication record and all Firestore data (topics, bookmarks, preferences) — is permanently and irreversibly deleted within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.
08

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) New

Pulse News complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). As a Data Fiduciary processing the personal data of Indian users, we are committed to upholding your rights under this law.

Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

📋 Right to Access You can request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the purposes for which it is processed.
✏️ Right to Correction You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data held by us.
🗑️ Right to Erasure You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time via Settings → Delete My Account.
📣 Right to Grievance Redressal You have the right to raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (see below) and receive a timely response.

Grievance Officer

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, we have designated a Grievance Officer to address concerns related to the processing of your personal data:

  • Name: Pulse News Team (Grievance Officer)
  • Email: team.pulsenews@gmail.com
  • Response time: We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

How to Raise a Complaint

To exercise any of your rights or raise a grievance under the DPDP Act 2023, please email us at team.pulsenews@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDP Privacy Request" and include:

  • Your registered email address or phone number (for account identification)
  • A description of your request or complaint
  • Any relevant details or supporting information

We will verify your identity before processing any request to protect your data security.

09

Children's Privacy

Pulse News is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately at team.pulsenews@gmail.com.

10

  • Request correction of inaccurate data

  • Opt out of personalised advertising via your device settings
  • Withdraw consent at any time (by deleting your account)
