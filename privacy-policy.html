Privacy Policy

Pulse News ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Pulse, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or phone number, we collect your email address, phone number, and display name.
  • Usage Data: Topics you follow, articles you read, and app preferences (theme, language).
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To personalize your news feed based on topics you follow
  • To sync your topics and bookmarks across devices
  • To manage your Premium subscription status
  • To send news notifications (only if you enable them)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Advertising

Pulse uses Google AdMob to show ads to free users. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data

Premium users do not see any ads. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase for the following:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure login via Google or phone number
  • Cloud Firestore: Storing your topics, bookmarks, and premium status
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Sending push notifications

Firebase's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Google's Firebase servers. We use industry-standard security measures. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete all local data using "Clear All Data" in Settings. To permanently delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Pulse News is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (via device settings)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of Pulse after changes means you accept the updated policy.
