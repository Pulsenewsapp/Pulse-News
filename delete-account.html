Delete Your Pulse Account

If you wish to delete your Pulse account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

Request via email

Send an email to team.pulsenews@gmail.com from the email address or phone number associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Include the following details:

• Your registered email address or phone number
• Reason for deletion (optional)

What gets deleted

Once your request is processed (within 7 business days), the following data will be permanently deleted:

• Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
• Your saved topics and bookmarks
• Your premium subscription status and history
• Any preferences and settings associated with your account

For questions, contact us at team.pulsenews@gmail.com.
